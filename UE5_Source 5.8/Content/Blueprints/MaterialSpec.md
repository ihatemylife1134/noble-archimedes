## Material Rarity Tiers
- **Common** (Materials 1-5): Drop from common enemies, found in candles
- **Rare** (Materials 6-40): Drop from bosses, rare enemies, or specific steal targets

## Forge Requirements
- Each weapon requires specific materials in specific quantities
- Materials are consumed on successful forge
- Failed forge attempts do not consume materials (but may lose time)
- Julia's Shop in Cordova Town is the primary forge location
