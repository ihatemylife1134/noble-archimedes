# Weapon Specification — 5 Types, 15 Weapons

This document specifies all weapons, their stats, and forge recipes,
based on the Bradygames guide weapon combining system.

## Weapon Type Characteristics

| Type | Feel | Range | Speed | Damage | Final Attacks |
|------|------|-------|-------|--------|---------------|
| Sword | Fast, balanced | Medium | Fast | Medium | Piercing, spinning hits |
| Axe | Slow, powerful | Short-Medium | Slow | High | Pop-up, shattering smash |
| Spear | Long range | Long | Medium | Medium-Low | Whirlwind, spinning, uppercut |
| Knuckles | Short range, fast combos | Short | Fast | Medium | Rapid punch, uppercut, breakdance |
| Special | Projectiles, novelty | Varies | Varies | Varies | Stagger enemies |

## Combo Attack Patterns
- **Sword**: Piercing thrusts, spinning hits
- **Axe**: Overhead smash, ground pound
- **Spear**: Thrust combos, whirlwind
- **Knuckles**: Rapid punches, uppercut, breakdance spin
- **Special**: Projectile barrages, stagger effects
